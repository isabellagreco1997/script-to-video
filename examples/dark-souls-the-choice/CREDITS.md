# Dark Souls: The Choice

First cut, 1:58. 1920 × 1080, 16:9, 30 fps.

The story of Dark Souls I, built around Gwyn’s sacrifice and the player’s final choice. Spoilers for the base game and both endings.

## Production

Created using the local [script-to-video skill](https://github.com/isabellagreco1997/script-to-video): Kokoro narration, word alignment, a word-anchored timeline, extracted gameplay frames, the HTML stage, encoding and visual audits. The stage was adapted for bounded frame loading, protected widescreen images and quieter typography.

Voice: local Kokoro `bm_george`. Original ambient music and explanatory diagrams made for this cut. The separate SRT can be uploaded to YouTube; captions are not burned over the images.
